1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the VSM - Virtual Store Manager mobile application (Android package: com.exofters.vetmate), web application, and related services operated by Exofters (Pvt) Ltd ("we", "us", or "our").

VSM is a Point of Sale (POS), inventory tracking, digital billing, customer credit (Khata), supplier management, and business accounting tool designed for diverse commercial setups, including but not limited to:

  • Pharmacies & Medical Stores
  • Supermarkets, Grocery & Karyana Stores
  • Mobile Phones, Electronics & Computer Repair Shops
  • Fertilizers, Pesticides, Seeds & Agro-Vet Supply Stores
  • Garments, Shoes & Fashion Boutiques
  • Hardware, Sanitary & Auto Spare Parts Dealers
  • Cafes, Restaurants & Food Counters
  • Wholesale & Distribution Centers

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide seamless point-of-sale operations, cloud synchronization, invoice printing, and inventory analytics:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Store Owner Information: Full name, business/store name, email address, contact telephone number, physical store address, and currency/tax preferences.
  • Staff & Cashier Accounts: Assigned staff names, email credentials, and administrative role permissions (e.g., Cashier, Store Admin).
  • Authentication Credentials: Secure hashed credentials managed via Google Firebase Authentication.

B. Commercial & Operational Data (Customer & Store Records)

  • Inventory & Product Catalog: Item names, SKU/barcodes, formula/generic names, categories, purchase cost, retail/selling price, current stock count, batch numbers, manufacturing/expiry dates, and item photos.
  • Sales & Digital Receipts: Invoice numbers, itemized line items, payment methods (Cash, Card, Digital, Credit/Udhaar), date/time stamps, discounts applied, and tax calculations.
  • Customer Ledger (Khata / Credit): Customer name, phone number, address, unpaid credit balances, payment logs, and receipt history.
  • Supplier Records & Purchase Orders: Supplier names, contact numbers, outstanding balances, received inventory bills, and payment records.
  • Operating Expenses: Business expense categories (rent, utilities, salaries, transport) and daily petty cash notes recorded by the user.

C. Device & Technical Diagnostics

  • Diagnostic Data: App version, operating system release, crash logs, and anonymized performance metrics to help us troubleshoot technical glitches and optimize UI rendering.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

To deliver advanced Point of Sale capabilities, VSM requests specific operating system permissions. We adhere strictly to the principle of least privilege:

Android Permission Category Specific Purpose in VSM
CAMERA Hardware Enables the built-in barcode and QR scanner for rapid checkout and product lookup, and allows snapping photos of inventory items. The camera is never accessed in the background or without explicit user initiation.
BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT Connectivity Enables discovery and communication with wireless ESC/POS thermal receipt printers (58mm / 80mm) to print customer sales invoices.
READ / WRITE STORAGE Files & Media Allows downloading and saving generated PDF receipts, sales invoices, Excel balance sheets, and inventory backup files to your local device storage.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Alerts Delivers timely notifications regarding low inventory quantities, upcoming product expiry dates, and essential account security notices.
BACKGROUND_RUNNER / WAKE_LOCK Automation Runs periodic on-device background evaluations (every 15–30 minutes) to verify if any product has fallen below its low-stock threshold, notifying store staff before stock runs out.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Point of Sale Execution: Calculating cart totals, applying discounts, updating inventory balances in real time, and generating print-ready receipts.
  • Real-time Cloud Sync: Synchronizing data between your smartphone, tablet, desktop browser, and multi-staff terminals.
  • Financial & Profit Insights: Generating visual charts depicting daily/weekly sales trends, net profits, inventory valuation, and top-selling items.
  • Stock Depletion Prevention: Generating batch warnings and low-stock alerts before shelves run empty.
  • Customer Account & Credit Records: Allowing store owners to track customer debt (*udhaar*) and share digital receipts via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Customer Support: Assisting you when you open support tickets or request data troubleshooting.

5. Data Storage, Security & Infrastructure

We treat business records with utmost confidentiality and implement enterprise-grade protection:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: VSM is powered by Google Firebase (hosted on secure Google Cloud Platform data centers).
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and our cloud databases are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: All stored database documents and cloud media files are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Multi-Tenant Isolation: Every store operates within an isolated tenant environment protected by granular Firestore Security Rules. No store owner can access or view another store's inventory, customers, or financial records.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted enterprise cloud infrastructure providers to provide our services. These providers only process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform: Authentication, Cloud Firestore (Real-time Database), Firebase Storage, and Cloud Messaging. (Google Privacy Policy)

We do NOT integrate third-party ad networks, behavioral trackers, or data-broker SDKs.

7. Your Rights & Data Portability

As a store owner and registered user, you retain complete rights over your data:

  • Access & Review: You can view all your stored inventory, customer accounts, and historical sales transactions at any time within the app.
  • Data Export: You can export your inventory catalog, transaction logs, and customer ledger to Excel (.xlsx) or PDF format directly from the app.
  • Correction & Updates: You can edit product specifications, customer details, and store profile parameters at any moment.
  • Revocation of Permissions: You can revoke device permissions (Camera, Bluetooth, Notifications) anytime through your device's operating system settings.

8. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

⚠️ Complete Control Over Your Account & Data
In compliance with Google Play Store User Data and Account Deletion policies, you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated store data at any time.

You can initiate deletion through either of the following methods:

Method 1: In-App Self-Service Deletion

  1. Open the VSM app and navigate to Settings.
  2. Tap on Account / Profile Details.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account & All Store Data.
  4. Confirm your authentication. Once confirmed, all your products, customer records, receipts, and images will be permanently erased.

Method 2: Email / Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can submit an account deletion request by emailing us at:

📧 adeel.shhid@gmail.com or info@exofters.site with the subject line "VSM Account Deletion Request" from your registered account email.

Upon verification, all account records, stores, sales histories, and uploaded images will be permanently purged from our active databases within 30 days.

9. Data Retention Policy

  • Active Accounts: We retain your business data as long as your account remains active, ensuring seamless business continuity and historical reporting.
  • Deleted Accounts: Upon confirmed deletion, all primary operational records are purged immediately from production databases. Residual encrypted backup snapshots are permanently overwritten within 30 to 60 days.
  • Legal & Tax Records: If required by applicable national or local commercial laws, certain anonymized transaction records may be retained solely to fulfill statutory legal obligations.
